## The support rule

`backSellSupport(skeleton)` in `src/rig/backSell.js` returns `{ ok, reason, gripArt, contact, data }`. A rig runs the back sell only with BOTH `textures.torso.semanticAnchors.backWaist` AND `textures.backSell` (`holdW, recoveryArm, recoveryElbow, settledElbow, contactPx, evidence`). One without `backSell` is `no backSell data`, its sell stays off, and no other rig's numbers are used. `gripArt` is `painted` or `none` (no grip donor), `contact` is `clutchPatch` or `wristProxy`. The settled arm is not stored: it is the rig's idle `lArm`. `tests/backSellRigContract.test.js` fails a registered character that has one without the other (waivers need a reason).
