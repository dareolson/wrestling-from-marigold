# Codex to Opus: current get-up issues

2026-10-27 · Codex · requested by Derek. Reviewed PLAN_GETUPS, George amendments, T1.0 at `7f255a6`, current rise/sell code and G10a measurements. No runtime edits or visual verdict; Opus directs execution.

My opinion: moving to authored get-ups is the right next experiment. The current issues involve support, turning and hand-route continuity together; another isolated hip or hand adjustment would not establish a coherent route. Keeping George's amplitude tuning parked is reasonable. However, a new rise cannot establish that the earlier lying leg peaks or thigh occlusion are fixed: retain those as separate observations at G4. Derek's G9 approval stands; do not reopen the latch work merely because its measurements are imperfect.

Before Sonnet goes further, I recommend clarifying these contracts:

1. **Separate D7 behavior comparisons from rise comparisons.** The films rule demands identical sell plans, but getups=v1 now intentionally removes rolls. Stage 5 cannot both use the original G9 roll plan and obey D7 in normal play. Use two comparisons: (a) complete old/new behavior with the differing sell plans labelled intentional; (b) controlled, identical supine/prone starting poses, hold offsets, pain and stamina for the rise alone. Keep a direct prone-landing case: removing rolls reduces exposure to the broken prone route but does not repair it. Stage 3's old roll-swap metric should be historical evidence, not a requirement to reintroduce a sell roll.

2. **Choose the route once; define what retargeting may change.** D4 selects an eligible entry using needsTurn at rise start, but also re-decides needsTurn until flipAt. What happens when a no-turn entry has already been chosen and the opponent crosses? Swapping to a different table mid-rise can jump; a no-turn entry may have no flipAt at all. Prefer a fixed route with explicitly authored optional turn branches at a common matching pose, or a documented frozen selection policy. Add the crossing cases to this plan, including crosses twice and an opponent inside the deadband. “Every get-up ends facing the opponent” also needs the previously accepted exception for a crossing after the turn window.

3. **Quick rises need an explicit duration/gameplay decision.** `_startQuickRise` currently takes 160 ms; `_startRiseUp` takes 850 + 750*hurt ms. The new entry's duration at full stamina leaves their mapping unspecified. Do not silently compress a supported knee rise into 160 ms or lengthen the quick-rise vulnerability window. Specify whether quick rise keeps its existing route during the first gate, receives its own authored fast entry, or changes gameplay with Derek's approval. Record actual milliseconds, not only normalized t.

4. **A planted pivot requires actor-position bookkeeping.** T1.3 correctly improves on a hip pivot, but preserving a knee's screen position may require an actor x correction. Say where that correction lives and whether collision, opponent distance and rope bounds follow it. Avoid a permanent visual offset between skeleton and wrestler. Measure the other planted foot too: preserving one point cannot guarantee both contacts remain fixed. Test a frame step that crosses flipAt without landing on it exactly, and interruption after the correction.

5. **Make the support schedule part of each entry.** D1's optional `hands` is not yet a concrete contract for D3. Specify target space (mat world point or thigh-local anchor), push window, reach weight and release window. For the backSell variants, explicitly assign the near hand to protection and far hand to support so generic hands cannot overwrite the carried reach. Check the actually drawn hold→first-rise and last-rise→standing frames, including contact state and masks, not just table endpoints. Post-IK/knee-limit poses are the measurements Derek sees.

6. **Selection must allow only one eligible route.** Seeded no-repeat selection needs previous id and random state/input in the pure function contract. Filter eligibility first; avoid the previous route only if another valid candidate remains. Never select an incompatible route just to meet no-repeat. nearRopes has no rope route in this round, so it must not eliminate every choice. Give the empty set an explicit safe fallback and test one/zero eligible entries.

I would keep Stage 1 small and get the first supported `backKneeHurt` prototype in front of Derek before expanding selection. The pose-sheet gate is useful, but the weight/turn judgment needs its normal-speed transition. No added asset generation or broader animation-system rewrite is indicated by this review.
