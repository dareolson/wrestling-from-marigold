# Codex review for Opus: Rev 4 / Rev 4b

2026-10-27 · Codex · requested by Derek. Read-only review of runtime and current uncommitted plan/probe at HEAD `00ffe63`; no runtime changes or visual approval. Opus directs, Sonnet executes. These are recommendations, not replacement decisions.

1. **Keep the G9 latch defect explicitly open.** Rev 4 addresses legs and occlusion, but not the early grip selection or recovery chatter reported at G9. In `Wrestler._syncSellAppearance`, grounded clutch selects grip by phase and seeds the latch on; only hold/rise/recovery use distance. Thus the early engagement is not necessarily a `stepGripLatch` threshold failure. Also the measured contact depends on the active texture, so switching textures changes the input that decides the next switch. Before tuning dwell/thresholds, log phase, active variant, latch state and both candidate contact positions against the same solved pose. Consider a consistent candidate-contact measurement and separate enter/exit thresholds if feedback is confirmed. Derek approved the forearm switch; retain this as a measured limitation unless he wants it fixed now.

2. **T11.3: fail closed on unavailable measurements.** `silhouetteDistance` returns Infinity for an unusable transform or silhouette; pure `handClearance` currently accepts that via `min >= marginPx`. The browser wrapper guards a missing torso silhouette, but not an unusable transform. With three occluders, explicitly require every required measurement to be usable and finite, with a test for a missing thigh/transform. Do not silently drop an unreadable occluder. Four hand samples also do not prove the entire painted hand is clear: retain the rendered overlap probe as the acceptance evidence, and inspect the frame immediately after each swap.

3. **T11.2: validate mask transforms and lifecycle before filming the package.** Copies must preserve world transforms, origins, frame, flips and alpha in the filter camera, in both facings. Ensure mask copies do not appear in the main scene and resources are reused and destroyed with the skeleton. Match the alpha cutoff used by the overlap measurement to the intended mask behavior, or document the difference. Measure cost during active masking after warmup, against D11 with the same scene/camera; report median and a tail measure, not only a single average.

4. **D12/T10.2 needs one application point and explicit scope.** D12 now covers all supine offsets and both halves of a roll; T10.2 still says back clutch and hold only, and T10.1 still says STOP if the source is not clutch despite its completed attribution to multiple sources. Align those instructions before execution. Scale sampled offsets before adding the lying base and before knee limiting, without mutating shared keys. `_startRiseUp` captures `_lyingHold().offsets`: ensure it captures the same scaled offsets that were actually drawn, or hold-to-rise can jump. Test roll midpoint continuity rather than assuming scaling both halves preserves it (their bases differ). Also state whether non-back injury sells on George are intentionally scaled.

5. **Rev 4b: hip continuity alone is insufficient.** A hip-centred sprite mirror can still teleport planted feet, knees and the protecting hand. For A/B, report both feet's world displacement at the flip, contact-hand displacement and waist-contact error, alongside the existing per-part steps. Require planted-foot stability during lean/lift and hand-to-knee contact only during its intended push window; target distance alone does not show weight transfer. A short normal-speed prototype of A should precede polishing B so Derek can reject the mirror treatment early.

6. **Clarify turn policy and the flag contract.** D14 says no-turn uses today's route, while D15 adds weight to all rises; specify whether no-turn gets D15. Define which opponent position is sampled and what happens if the opponent crosses during the rise: start-time choice can be stale at standing, allowing `move()` to flip again. Test crossover, near-equal x, interruptions and reset. Define an explicit frozen target or bounded retarget policy rather than assuming the standing re-face disappears. Keep the protective-hand and depth state coherent across a facing change.

7. **Use matched evidence and keep gates separate.** G9 explicitly used different random rolls; regenerate the before with the same seed and verify the actual sell plan matches, not just the seed (`aftermath_film.mjs` already seeds Math.random). Rev 4 G10 question 3 proposes promote/push, but Rev 4b introduces another pending gate: state exactly which change/flag a G10 approval would promote. Update Status/Done means when Opus settles scope; they still describe earlier HEAD/gates and G6 completion.
