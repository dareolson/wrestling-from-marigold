## Advice before accepting the measurements

1. **Distinguish samples from painted pixels.** `getups_route.mjs:MEASURE` computes “lowest” from `nearHandSamples` (wrist, contact, distal point, tip). The tip is a centroid of the bottom alpha rows, not the lowest world-space pixel after rotation. Thus “lowest drawn pixel above the hem every frame” is not established. Likewise the 12 thigh-touch frames are sample intersections, not visible leaking pixels. Label those as sampled geometry; use a lossless rendered isolation/alpha overlap check on the worst frames to establish actual hem crossing and visible thigh leakage. JPEG films are for viewing, not exact alpha counting.

2. **Diagnose the union mask with a forced overlap control.** One frame changing by 9 px may mean the torso already hides most intersections, or that geometry samples differ from painted coverage; it does not by itself prove the mask is broken. Render the mask source alone and a forced hand-over-thigh pose with torso-only and union masks. Verify both thighs in both facings. In `handUnionSource`, copies use local x/y/scale/rotation and do not copy alpha, despite the world-transform/alpha contract in A3. Establish that parents are identity in the current rigs, copy alpha, and test any supported parent transforms before claiming that contract met. Include the outstanding active-mask median/p95 cost before promotion.

3. **Measure the returned IK pose.** `solveRouteHand` measures `err` at the beginning of each pass, then can modify p on the final pass and return that newer pose with the previous error. Re-measure the actual final drawn pose for the reported residual. Its updateUpright calls also mutate skeleton rendering state three times; include that real cost in the performance comparison, not just mask cost. No need for a new solver unless the final-pose evidence shows a failure.

4. **Do not omit movement precisely at the pivot.** The film tool comment says steps are body-relative, but implementation sets wristStep/tipStep to null when x changes, rather than subtracting actor displacement. The summaries then replace some missing values with zero. Report world displacement for every consecutive frame; optionally report body-relative displacement separately. Missing measurements should remain missing with an explicit count. This matters because the earlier 52–79 px ankle steps and 6–8 px knee drift are still recorded limitations, not closed by the absence of another complaint. Ask Derek about them in the existing gate without implying they are automatically blockers.
