## R3 Grab the rope (design first)

- **T3.0 Derek decides (Opus writes the options page, no code).** Candidates:
  1. *Reach for the rope in a hold.* Today's rope breaks are automatic. Instead the held man must reach: a hand travels to the nearest strand and grips it; the break comes on contact. Works for every wall, not just the sides.
  2. *Hold the ropes to block.* A man near a wall holds a strand to stop an Irish whip, a slam or a suplex (counter, the attacker's move fails).
  3. *Pull up on the ropes.* Getting up near a wall uses the strands (ties into the get-up library).
  4. *Hang on the ropes.* Dazed draped over the middle rope (sets up moves like a running knee).
  Each lists: input, AI use, which moves it touches, cost. Derek picks.
