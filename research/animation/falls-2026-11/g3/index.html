Misses first

  • The pick has never been looked at by anyone but its numbers. This page shows what each rule picks and why; whether a sitBack off a jab, a kneesForward off a knee lift or a spill off a shove reads right is Derek's call. Not claimed: that any of it reads right.
  • In ordinary play almost no strike knocks a man down. The knockdown needs the hit to leave him under 35 stamina, and stamina comes back within seconds: two 150 s AI-vs-AI runs at full stamina logged zero strike falls (no headbutt landed either time). The match clip below therefore holds both men's stamina at 36 every frame, a worn-down match on demand, and says so. It is not a measured rate.
  • "From behind" is a staged case. A strike turns the receiver to face the attacker, so the from-behind rule only runs when his facing is held (waistlock, a turn beat, a run). The table holds his facing to show it (_facingLock); in the match clip the corner charge is the one that uses it (the corner is behind him).
  • Near the ropes only matters between 70 and 92 px. The lying lengths are 70 behind / 96 ahead (backward, sitBack, spill) and 92 behind / 84 ahead (forward, kneesForward), so a rope 85 px behind him rules the forward falls out and nothing else; closer than 70 px behind, every fall pokes through and the rule keeps the least overshoot (flagged "cramped"). No cell in this table needed it.
  • The shove spills toward the rope. With a rope 85 px behind him (plus the shove's 40 px push) the shove still spills: the spill lies along the same line as a back fall (70 behind), so it fits. Whether it should spill sideways in depth instead is a call for Derek; the spill's art is the one authored in T2.5e.
  • Spill (T3.3), four runs: clean. Lou and George, both facings, ?falls=v1 only (the &spill=1 flag is gone): no knee or elbow outside its limit, no clamp hit; D4 fall time over free fall from the standing pelvis: spill-george-l 1.06, spill-george-r 1.06, spill-lou-l 0.99, spill-lou-r 0.99. The near arm's brace is in the fall's keys, before contact, so the audit (which starts at contact) classes the hands "stayed" / "released, replanted" for spill-george-l and "stayed" / "never supported" for spill-lou-l (the far arm flies up in the keys, so on Lou it never touches the mat in the landing).
  • Table totals: 48 cells, 38 falls (backward 4, forward 6, sitBack 17, kneesForward 7, spill 4), 10 staggers. Falls in a fresh cell: headbutt-fresh-george, headbutt-fresh-lou; a tired, behind or ropes cell that only staggered: none.
  • Not covered: falls from the throws are the G2 page's; the clothesline family still flips; the strike falls land at the default tier (no strike is a big move); the AI does not choose a fall, the situation does; the head and wrists have no channel; no wince face.
