Falls G3 r3: the wear meter (?falls=v1)

For Derek: knockdowns and exceptional landings now follow a separate wear meter (0-100): it rises with the damage a man takes, recovers slowly, resets at the start of a match and not on a get-up. Stamina still does everything else (pins, kickouts, begging off, staggers). ?wear=show draws the wear bar under the stamina bar (purple; orange past the knockdown line, red past the exceptional line; the two white ticks are the lines). Off by default.

Misses first

  • Strike knockdowns are fewer than "a few per match": 2, 0, 4, 1 per 150 s run (sum 7), first at 110 s, none, 84 s, 149 s. The AIs land only 9, 8, 7, 9 strikes a run, so the line decides few strikes; one run (george-lou-2) has none. The lower line (55, second row below) gives 12 but a first knockdown at 78 s, 91 s, 36 s, 56 s: 1 run under 45 s, which breaks the first target. I kept the line that meets it.
  • Wear-driven exceptional landings: 2 in the four runs (41.65s suplex wear 100; 52.3s bodySlam wear 100). The AI throws a suplex, body slam or back body drop rarely (none in 3 of the first 4 first-pass runs), so the line is checked by unit tests at the threshold and by these runs only where a throw happened. The ones that did land came early, at wear 100: a burst of damage in the first minute, not a late collapse. The piledriver is exceptional on its own (1 in these runs) and is not counted.
  • Runs vary a lot with the same constants: mean wear in the last 30 s ranges 30.5 to 78 across the four runs, because the AI mix differs (a run of holds or dodges is little damage). Four runs cannot show the spread; Stage 4's long run will.
  • A bout ending inside the 150 s resets the men (the arena resets wear with stamina at the next match), so a 150 s soak can contain two short matches; wear columns drop at a reset. Not retuned.
  • Clip: a continuous 150 s film is 9000 frames, so the clip keeps the windows around each mark (1.5 s before, 3.5 s after), each played at normal speed with the bars on; the rest of the run is in the wear trace table. 2 marks in this run (seed 1, lou-george).
  • Headbutt and corner charge still always fall; they are counted in their own column, not against the targets.
