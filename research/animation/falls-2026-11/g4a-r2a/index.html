The depth-conflict column (attacker parts drawn over his) peaks at 1410 px: that is the upper arm and head over him, the part to watch in the loops.

Ring room is clamped along the route (test), so close to the far rope the elbow misses the chest rather than the body leaving the ring.
  • Fast arrival: the blend and the reach move a joint 18-32 px per frame (the flight before it is 13-34); the reach is eased in on purpose, so the elbow arrives at speed. The seam at the end (settle into the lying pose, no handoff) is the last column.
  • The striking part is declared as the far arm's forearm + hand (the arm the stills used); the plan's note says "near-camera forearm + hand", but with the attacker on the far side it is the far arm that reaches the chest. Say if you meant the other arm.
  • Elbow drop only. Knee drop, diving elbow and top dive are untouched (their G4a path), and the whole of T4.7's full validation waits for this look.
  • Rig-wide not checked: the pose angles are one set for both rigs; George's longer arm leaves a different gap (elbow gap column). Single-run measurement, seeded; no soak.
