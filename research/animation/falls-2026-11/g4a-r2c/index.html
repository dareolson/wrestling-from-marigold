Left: the T4.7b drop (ba94014). Right: revised. Both start on the same frame (the call of the move); the hit lands on the same frame in both (44 frames after the call: Derek's timing is locked). The yellow tag names the mark on screen: apex, flight end, on the mat (current: the flight clip's last frame; revised: the hip reaching the contact height, which is the hit), HIT, roll-off, kicks start (revised only). Derek judges the look.

What changed: (1) the arms lead the fall: the striking arm reaches its cocked contact pose by 45% of the fall while the body keeps its u² gravity ease; (2) the stop is a yield: the hip sinks 14 px past contact over the first 60% of the hold (decelerating), then recoils; (3) his legs lie along the mat from contact through the roll-off, angled a little away up the screen, instead of the raised far boot and splayed near knee; (4) the receiver's new sell after a ground hit (groundHitSell, reusable by the knee drop and dives): chest pinned for 0.08 s, then head and shoulders curl up, knees draw up, arms flail, then three alternating leg kicks from 0.16 s that fade, back on the lying pose by 1.0 s. The jolt is not used for the elbow drop any more. Down timer and get-up unchanged.

Misses first

  • The body still reads fairly flat in the fall. The cocked arm leads, but the 2D rig has no roll about the long axis, so "on its side with the elbow lowest" is only the arm pose, not the torso. Say if it needs the torso tipped, or art.
  • The stop is softer but still short: hip speed goes 30 / 14 / ~1 px per frame over the hit frame and the next (George 34 / 16 / ~1); the squash is 6.4-8.5 px past the hit frame (was 2.4-3.1). A yield over 3-4 frames from 30 px/frame would need about 45 px of travel, so this is a compromise.
  • Supine receivers overlap the attacker more than before: his limbs on the attacker peak at 2559 px (was 2188), outside-limb overlap peaks at 2825 px (was 2,503), from the curl and the kicks. Prone receivers overlap less. Part of it is legitimate 2D occlusion of a man lying against another; I did not tune the sell down to hide it.
  • His kicks reach the attacker's side of the screen: no part of the receiver goes past the side ropes (0 px, all cells). The kick counts below are ankle peaks over 8 px, 2-4 per leg; the design is three alternating kicks, the rest are the knee draw and the arm flail moving the ankle.
  • His legs "kick" by swinging the whole leg in the grounded rig's channels; the feet go up the screen, not toward the camera, so on a supine man they can read as lifting more than kicking. Judge in motion.
  • The sell holds the pin off for 1.0 s (the jolt held it for about 0.55 s).
  • Seam at the end of the settle is 0.9-1.0 px (was 0.6-0.7). Carried over: the striking part is the far forearm, he ends about 130 px up-screen, Lou's contact pose is still roughly parallel to George. Elbow drop only; knee drop and dives wait; no soak.
