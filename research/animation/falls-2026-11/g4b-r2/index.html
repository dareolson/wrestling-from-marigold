Misses first

The rig's lying-on-back pose has the head on the far side, so the roll's second half is drawn with the facing mirrored and every angle negated: the joints do not move (the pose test checks no angle jumps), but the sprites flip at the apex, and his facing is the mirror of the dive's from then on, so he rises from his back facing the other way. If the art flip shows, the clean fix is a back-lying pose with the head forward (an art request); I have not asked for it.

Without it the stand-up snapped him round in one frame (39-60 px).
  • The tumble spins fast: a quarter turn in about 5 frames, the largest joint up to 49.03 px/frame; the roll's pivot up to 36.79. Smooth, no single-frame jump. The tumble goes 22 px above the mat at the top (the roll 28) so the head and feet clear it while inverted.
  • Halved (Derek, r3): the lift above the mat (roll 28 to 14 px, tumble 44 to 22) and the trip up the screen (he now travels about 42-47 px, was 74-94). The cost: he lands partly across the receiver. Overlap after landing is 553-984 px (was 0 at the full trip), and 39-55 frames overlap by more than 50 px from contact on. The landing y is the receiver's far edge minus a 34 px gap, scaled by 0.5 (route.scale); something near 0.75 would clear him again if it reads tangled.
