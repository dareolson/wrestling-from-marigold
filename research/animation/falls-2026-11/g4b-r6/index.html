Misses first

  • The straight moment stays. The art flips at the apex, and a knee bent before the flip would point backward after it, so the legs pass through straight there (knee bend under 0.2 rad for about 4 frames, u 0.43-0.58). What changed is where they point: r5 threw them across to the head's side, then swung them back while the torso tipped over (the arch). r6 keeps them on the far side the whole roll: thigh angle on screen -0.62, -0.60 at the flip, -1.20 at the tuck, -1.50 / -1.97 lying, one direction only.
  • The torso rises less: it peaks 0.59 rad short of vertical (2.55 rad; r5 3.0, 0.14 short). Legs straight under a vertical torso would put the feet through the mat at this height; the alternative is raising the apex, which Derek halved at r3.
  • After the flip, the tuck (thighs up, shins hanging, as the pivot) runs 6.7 px/frame for 5 frames (neighbouring frames 3-5); the last 3 frames before the landing reach 8.9 (the gravity fall, unchanged).
  • Feet clear the mat until the landing (closest 10 px at the start of the tuck, test leg lengths 44 + 44). Apex hip 70 (r5 66), the bounce lift is unchanged (14 px).
  • Not changed: the sprite flip itself, the get-up's turn inside the rise, overlap after landing, timing, travel.
