Gait G3: the run as its own gait

Normal-speed loops, fixed seed, both rigs, both facings. Stage 3 changes only the run (and the whip run): its own stride derived for 3.25 steps/s, stance .40 so both feet leave the mat (about 20% of the cycle), swing-foot lift 32, a run hip that is lowest at mid-stance and highest in flight, and at the rope turn the stride mirrors so the planted foot stays planted. Walk, lean and the stop are the Stage 2 values. These are measurements, not a verdict; the eye decides. Known miss: the whip start still flips facing in one frame (foot step 36 to 60 px at the start of the whip, Stage 4).
