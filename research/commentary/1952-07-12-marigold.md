### Marigold history

- The venue began as a prominent North Side German beer garden and restaurant.
- Its Marigold name dates to the World War I period, replacing the Bismarck name
  amid anti-German sentiment.
- Ballroom dancing declined in the late 1920s; amateur boxing began there in
  1929, with boxing promotion established by the mid-1930s.
- By 1952 it was strongly associated with televised boxing and wrestling.
- Fred Kohler moved his Marigold wrestling cards from Monday to Saturday nights
  in January 1952.
