# Commentary Research

This directory contains research, source notes, draft copy, and production
planning for the proposed historical commentary system. Nothing here is loaded
by the game or included in its runtime asset pipeline.

Research rules:

- Treat the fictional match date as a hard knowledge cutoff.
- Separate verified history, deliberate alternate history, and unverified leads.
- Keep a source URL beside every historical claim.
- Do not use facts learned only after the match date as announcer knowledge.
- Draft an original synthetic period announcer; do not imitate an identifiable
  real broadcaster.
- Historical copy is not approved for recording until its status is `verified`.
