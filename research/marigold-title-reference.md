## Revision brief

Before a historically grounded regeneration, obtain and inspect a period arena
exterior reference. Record its date, provenance, visible entrance, roofline,
window rhythm, and sign shape. Do not infer a theater marquee from a poster or
copy the neighboring Chateau Theater. Do not claim later church signage was
present in 1952.

Keep the 16:10 title composition, restrained ink-and-paint style, warm entrance
light against desaturated Chicago night, small human figures, dry summer street,
and editable lettering. Romantic emphasis can heighten the warmth, gathering
crowd, and sense of arrival while preserving referenced architecture.

If the historical sign lacks space for the whole game title, use a separate
editable title overlay rather than enlarging or inventing the physical sign.
Preserve v1 and save any revised artwork as a sibling version. An artist's
reconstruction of unresolved features must be labeled as interpretation.
